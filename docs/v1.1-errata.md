# JAAM v1.1 implementation contract

This document resolves behavior named but not fully defined in the locked v1.1
language specification. It is normative for the compiler.

## Lexical and argument rules

- Tuples contain exactly two or three expressions.
- Positional arguments cannot follow named arguments; names cannot repeat.
- Supported units are `m`, `cm`, `mm`, `um`, `nm`, `Hz`, `kHz`, `MHz`, `GHz`,
  `deg`, `rad`, and `ohm`. Values are normalized to SI.
- A bare length is metres, a bare angle is degrees, and bare zero is accepted in
  any numeric context.
- v1 expressions do not include arithmetic. Constant folding covers numeric
  literals, units, tuple components, and resolved call arguments.

## Simulation directives

- A program provides either `frequency` or both range directives, never both.
- `free_space` and `pml_8` mean six `PML_8` boundaries; `pec` and `mur` mean six
  `PEC` and `MUR` boundaries respectively.
- The simulation box is the geometry bounds plus one quarter of the shortest
  free-space wavelength on every side. PML consumes eight cells at its exterior.

## Geometry

- `box.at` is the minimum corner and all `size` components are positive.
- `helix(radius:, pitch:, turns:)` starts at `(radius, 0, 0)`, winds around `+z`,
  and advances by `pitch` per turn.
- `simple_arc` is counter-clockwise in the XY plane.
- `parabolic_arc` is in the XY plane and uses `x = y^2/(4f)` over the requested
  aperture.
- Curves are tessellated deterministically using the shortest wavelength.
- `concat` takes two or more earlier wire names, translates each later segment
  end-to-start without rotation, requires equal radius/material, and permits at
  most one feed. Referenced segments become construction-only.
- `revolve` performs a complete turn around `x`, `y`, or `z`; the earlier planar
  seed becomes construction-only.
- `curve()` is reserved and rejected in v1.

## Materials, wires, and feeds

- `pec`, `copper`, and `gold` are PEC-style conductors.
- `earth_dry` has relative permittivity 4 and conductivity 0.001 S/m. Ground
  boxes are extended laterally to the inner truncation boundary.
- Unknown materials are errors.
- A wire is thin when `radius / shortest_wavelength < 0.02`. This cutoff keeps
  the specification's canonical 1.5 mm, 2.45 GHz Yagi elements on the required
  thin-wire path. Thin wires lower to
  `AddCurve`; other wires lower to radius-bearing `AddWire` geometry.
- A feed is placed at the wire's arc-length midpoint. Its gap is the smaller of
  one tenth of the path and `max(2 * radius, wavelength / 1000)`, and its lumped
  port follows the dominant component of the local tangent.
